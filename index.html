About Me

A brief look into who I am, what drives my research, and what I'm looking for next

Background

I'm a Machine Learning Engineer and Security Researcher with a passion for building intelligent systems that are both powerful and resilient against adversarial threats. My journey has taken me through competitive cybersecurity — placing in multiple national CTF competitions — while simultaneously deepening my expertise in applied machine learning. I've completed my bachelor's studies in Computer Science from Abdul Wali Khan University Mardan with an impressive ranking of top 3% in the class.

AI Engineering

I design and build intelligent AI applications that solve real-world problems, with a focus on Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, AI agents, and workflow automation.

Generative-AI & LLMs

Fine-tuning · Prompt engineering

Building production-grade applications powered by large language models — from fine-tuning and prompt engineering to designing reliable, scalable inference pipelines that turn LLM capabilities into real product features.

RAG & Multi-Agent Systems

Vector search · Agent coordination

Building retrieval-augmented and multi-agent architectures that combine grounded knowledge with coordinated, autonomous reasoning. I design RAG pipelines that retrieve accurate, up-to-date context to reduce hallucination, and multi-agent systems where specialized agents plan, delegate, and verify each other's work.

AI Agents & Workflow Automation

Multi-agent systems · Tool use

Developing autonomous and semi-autonomous agents that plan, use tools, and complete multi-step tasks — automating complex workflows and integrating AI into real business processes end to end.

Research Interests

My research spans machine learning security, adversarial robustness, and applied cryptography — with hands-on work across competitive cybersecurity, threat detection systems, and AI safety.

Adversarial Machine Learning

Robustness · Evasion attacks

Studying how machine learning models fail under adversarial pressure, and designing defenses that hold up against evasion, poisoning, and model-extraction attacks. I'm interested in the trade-offs between model robustness, accuracy, and real-world deployability.

Privacy & Security in LLMs

Red Teaming LLMs · Data leakage

Investigating how large language models can be compromised — through prompt injection, jailbreaking, or leakage of sensitive training data — and researching defenses that keep them safe to deploy. My focus spans robust alignment techniques, input/output sanitization, and privacy-preserving methods for training and inference that protect both users and the underlying data.

AI for Cybersecurity

Threat detection · Automated defense

Applying machine learning to strengthen defensive security operations — from detecting anomalies and intrusions in real time to automating vulnerability discovery and threat triage. I'm interested in how AI systems can proactively identify emerging attack patterns and reduce response time, while remaining robust enough to avoid becoming an attack surface themselves.
